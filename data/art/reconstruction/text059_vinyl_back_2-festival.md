### Festival and Location Details: Oregon Eclipse 2017 (Sun Stage)

This photograph depicts the **Sun Stage** at the **Oregon Eclipse 2017 (Global Eclipse Gathering)**, held at **Big Summit Prairie** in Crook County, Oregon, USA.

* **Documentation & Event Review:** **[The 2017 Oregon Eclipse Gathering: An Event in Totality](https://consciouselectronic.com/2017/09/12/the-2017-oregon-eclipse-gathering-an-event-in-totality-event-review/)** (Conscious Electronic Review & Photos).

### Key Visual Evidence
* **Signature Art Structures:** Pagoda-like wooden pillars topped with flat, psychedelic fabric umbrella canopies, custom-designed for the Sun Stage by the Alchemy Arts Collective (artist Fez Gaetz).
* **Stage Mandala:** Intricate wooden mandala backdrop surrounding the central stage LED screen.
* **Environment:** Surrounded by the mature pine tree line of Ochoco National Forest bordering Big Summit Prairie.

### Venue Analysis & Excluded Locations
* **Boom Festival (Portugal):** Boom's main stage (Dance Temple) utilizes continuous tensile fabric roofs covering the entire dance floor rather than independent tiered umbrella pillars, and features a more arid landscape.
* **Ozora Festival (Hungary):** Features a singular thatched-roof structure or bio-dome main stage rather than open-air pillar canopies.
* **Lightning in a Bottle / Do LaB (California):** Features sweeping, continuous fabric cones rather than flat-topped tiered wooden pillars.
