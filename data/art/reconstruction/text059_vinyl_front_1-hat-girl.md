### Festival and Location Details: Oregon Eclipse 2017

This photograph was taken at the **Oregon Eclipse 2017 (Global Eclipse Gathering)** at **Big Summit Prairie** in Crook County, Oregon, USA.

* **Documentation & Event Review:** **[The 2017 Oregon Eclipse Gathering: An Event in Totality](https://consciouselectronic.com/2017/09/12/the-2017-oregon-eclipse-gathering-an-event-in-totality-event-review/)** (Conscious Electronic Review & Photos).

### Key Visual Evidence & Venue Analysis
* **Terrain and Dust:** Fine, powdery high-desert dust characteristic of Big Summit Prairie in Central Oregon during late August, ruling out European dairy farm venues (such as Glastonbury).
* **Festival Aesthetic:** Wide-brimmed sun hat, black bohemian layering, and utilitarian tote bag, matching West Coast transformational festival attire produced by Symbiosis Gatherings.
* **Roaming Performers:** Elaborate pink and orange feathered costumes worn by roaming performance troupes and global samba ensembles featured across the festival grounds.
* **Art Infrastructure:** Tall, colorful pennant flags and distant multi-tiered fabric/timber shade canopies erected across the prairie.
