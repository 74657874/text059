### Landmark and Location Details

The landmark in the photograph is **[Cathedral Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_Rock)** in **Sedona, Arizona**.

* **Location:** **[Cathedral Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_Rock)**, Yavapai County, Arizona, USA (within **Coconino National Forest**).
* **Vantage Point:** Photographed from the **Red Rock Crossing** area along Oak Creek.
* **Geology & Environment:** Carved from the red sandstone of the Permian Schnebly Hill Formation, surrounded by dense Upper Sonoran pinyon-juniper woodland and pine forest.
