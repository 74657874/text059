### Exhibition and Location Details

This photograph captures the **"World in One Room"** ceramics installation.

* **Location:** **[Victoria and Albert Museum (V&A)](https://www.vam.ac.uk)**, London, United Kingdom.
* **Gallery:** **[Ceramics Galleries (Room 137 / G6)](https://collections.vam.ac.uk/search/?id_gallery=THES49876&page=1&page_size=15&q=Room+137)**.
* **History & Documentation:** **[History of the Ceramics Galleries and Ceramic Staircase](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/history-of-the-ceramics-galleries-and-ceramic-staircase?srsltid=AfmBOooRFeCGQIcLYFlSG-HFNVcoIsiBsoGOreejczz8B9_Cs-CHa9iY)**.
* **Details:** Features an extensive, dense display of antique porcelain figurines, vessels, and upside-down teacup arrangements set within mirrored display cases that create an infinite reflection effect.
